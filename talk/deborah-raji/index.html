Deborah Raji — Safety, by any other name: Towards a sociotechnical view on AI Safety

UC Berkeley

Tuesday, December 2, 2025, 4:30 PM PT · 621 Sutardja Dai Hall

AI Safety is now a common term, used in a variety of regulatory, education and funding contexts. However, most formulations of 'AI safety' fail to capture a coherent picture of the socio-technical nature of AI systems, simultaneously under-estimating and over-estimating certain risks and impacts. In this talk, I’ll share the perspective of those hoping to adopt this socio-technical view, and the implications for AI development, adoption and policymaking.
