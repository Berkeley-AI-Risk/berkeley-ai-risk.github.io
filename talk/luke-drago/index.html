Luke Drago — The Labor Question: How the Politics of AI-Driven Labor Displacement Could Shape Transformative AI

Workshop Labs

Tuesday, February 17, 2026, 4:00 PM PT · 621 Sutardja Dai Hall

As transformative AI approaches, the power of regular people over government and society may dramatically decrease. Where does this power come from, how does AI shift it, and what can we do about it? This talk will argue that labor disruption will be the driving force behind AI-driven political action, and that there could be a window where significant displacement arrives before a sudden takeoff in AI capabilities. This window is the best opportunity to shape AI development, but it won’t stay open forever.
