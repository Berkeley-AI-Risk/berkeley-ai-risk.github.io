Jacob Steinhardt — Building the public tech stack for overseeing AI

UC Berkeley

Tuesday, March 17, 2026, 4:00 PM PT · 621 Sutardja Dai Hall

How AI will affect society is driven by the dynamics underlying AI development—the constraints, incentives, and oversight under which AI systems operate. A powerful lever to shape these dynamics is scalable measurement: technology that creates high-quality, systematic public understanding of important properties of AI systems. In this talk, we will discuss three forms of scalable measurement: scalable oversight of AI agent transcripts, scalable discovery of long-tail behaviors, and scalable explanation of language model internal states. In each case, an important component is using AI to understand AI: building AI-driven tools to investigate other AI systems, thus allowing us to turn compute into understanding.
